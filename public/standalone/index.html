How the Minimax AI Works

This game features an autonomous game-playing AI powered by the Minimax Decision Rule. When it is the AI’s turn, it constructs a complete state-space game tree of all remaining legal moves, evaluates every terminal outcome (AI Win: +10, Human Win: -10, Draw: 0), and picks the branch that strictly maximizes its minimum guaranteed payoff. Because Minimax plays optimally, the AI is mathematically unbeatable.
